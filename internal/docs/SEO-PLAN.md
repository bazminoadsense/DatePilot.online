# DatePilot SEO Plan

Canonical structure separates the main content architecture from trust/support pages:

- Main pages: `/`, `/calculators`, `/calendar`, `/time`, `/guides/<slug>`, and `/faq`.
- Calculator pages: `/calculators/<slug>`, `/calendar/<slug>`, and `/time/<slug>`.
- Trust/support pages: `/about`, `/contact`, `/privacy-policy`, `/cookie-policy`, `/terms`, `/disclaimer`, and `/report-an-error`.
- Technical files: `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, and the application 404 state.

The 30 main pages are a usefulness target, not a thin-content quota. Trust, legal, and support pages are required in addition to those 30 and must not be counted as SEO articles. Avoid indexable input permutations.

Every page needs a unique title, description, H1, canonical URL, useful introductory copy, descriptive headings, internal links, and accurate metadata. Provide sitemap and robots files. Use structured data only when it describes visible content. Do not scrape, mass-produce thin pages, create doorway pages, or use hidden text.

The keyword evidence, cluster map, content gaps, and initial briefs are maintained in [KEYWORD-RESEARCH.md](KEYWORD-RESEARCH.md). Metrics must remain unknown until verified with a keyword research tool or Search Console; do not manufacture volume, KD, CPC, or ranking data.

## Backlink strategy

Earn links through useful resources, not volume or paid placement schemes:

- Publish genuinely reference-worthy guides such as ISO weeks, leap years, working days, and timezone behavior.
- Offer original worked examples and methodology that educators, planners, and technical writers can cite.
- Submit the site only to relevant, reputable directories or communities where DatePilot is genuinely useful.
- Build relationships with calendar, productivity, education, travel, and developer communities through helpful contributions rather than promotional link drops.
- Reclaim legitimate mentions and report inaccurate or broken references when appropriate.
- Never buy links, exchange links at scale, automate outreach spam, or use private blog networks.
- Track referring domains and link quality, but do not make backlink count a publishing target.
