The keyword candidates below are intent hypotheses to validate before publication. They are based on DatePilot's documented tools and user problems, not claimed search-volume data. Do not treat the list as proof of demand or competition.

## Decision rules

- Create a page only when the query represents a distinct user problem and DatePilot can provide useful original value.
- Consolidate synonyms and near-identical calculator searches into one strong canonical page.
- Prefer calculator plus explanation when the visitor needs an answer immediately.
- Prefer a guide or reference page when the visitor needs rules, definitions, or a worked method.
- Reject pages that differ only by a date, year, city, number, or keyword synonym.
- Validate candidates with Google Search Console, a reputable keyword tool, and a manual SERP review before committing production content.

## Validation protocol for every candidate

Before creating or expanding a page, record the following from a keyword tool and manual search review:

- Primary and secondary queries.
- Country, language, and date of the measurement.
- Monthly volume, KD, CPC if relevant, and source URL. Use `Unknown - requires keyword research tool` when unavailable.
- The first-page result types and whether the SERP answers the question.
- Repeated gaps such as missing formulas, unclear endpoint rules, outdated timezone explanations, or poor calculator UX.
- Existing DatePilot page overlap and the consolidation decision.
- The approved page type, canonical URL, and internal links.

## Content gaps to investigate

These are hypotheses to validate against live results, not claims that competitors fail:

1. Calculator pages that state whether endpoints are included.
2. Calendar-month arithmetic that explains what happens to dates such as the 31st.
3. Working-day calculations that clearly separate weekends from optional holidays.
4. ISO week examples around the December/January boundary.
5. Time-zone explanations that show local date changes, not just offset numbers.
6. DST examples that explain why elapsed hours and clock readings can differ.
7. Date-format guidance for international teams that shows ambiguous examples and unambiguous alternatives.
8. A correction workflow that makes calculator accuracy accountable.

## Publishing gate

A candidate is not approved merely because it appears in this table. Before publication, attach a keyword-tool export or Search Console evidence, a SERP notes record, a content brief, calculation tests, an originality review, and an overlap decision. If the evidence is weak or the page would be near-duplicate, reject or consolidate it.
