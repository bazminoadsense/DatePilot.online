# DatePilot AdSense Compliance Rulebook

This is an internal quality rulebook, not legal advice or a guarantee of approval. Google evaluates the whole site and has no simple page-count formula.

## 1. What Google requires

DatePilot must provide original, high-quality and useful pages, clear navigation, policy-compliant content and traffic, site ownership/access, supported content and language, and a fully built site. Google may consider insufficient content, quality, policy, navigation, traffic, or language issues.

## 2. What DatePilot can publish

Every page needs a genuine date/time purpose, original explanation, accurate methodology or formula, checked examples, useful context, relevant related tools, and clear assumptions or limitations. Do not copy, scrape, or lightly rewrite material.

## 3. What DatePilot must not publish

Do not publish or monetize illegal, dangerous or derogatory, sexually explicit, deceptive, malicious, fraudulent, copyright-infringing, or search-manipulative content. Distinguish policy violations from publisher restrictions: some sensitive categories may receive limited or no ads rather than being outright prohibited. Check current Google Publisher Policies before release.

## 4. How DatePilot creates content

Do not write a page-count target such as “publish 500 articles because AdSense likes lots of content.” Begin with a real user problem. Calculator pages explain the task, inputs, result, methodology, assumptions, examples, limitations, and related needs. Automation may assist drafting or testing, but a human reviewer owns accuracy, originality, and usefulness.

## 5. Pre-AdSense approval checklist

- [ ] Website is fully built
- [ ] No coming-soon or unfinished pages
- [ ] Core tools work
- [ ] Original content on important pages
- [ ] No copied, scraped, thin, or automatically generated pages
- [ ] Clear navigation and logical categories
- [ ] Mobile friendly and accessible
- [ ] No deceptive UI or prohibited content
- [ ] Privacy Policy, Terms, Contact, and About pages
- [ ] Important links, 404, sitemap, robots.txt, HTTPS, and Search Console work
- [ ] No artificial, paid-to-click, paid-to-surf, autosurf, or click-exchange traffic
- [ ] No asking users to click ads
- [ ] Ads will not interfere with tools
- [ ] Site provides genuine value without ads

No content volume or implementation can guarantee approval. Google makes the final decision.
