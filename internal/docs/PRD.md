# DatePilot Product Requirements

DatePilot.online is a focused date, calendar, and time utility platform for calculating, understanding, comparing, and working with dates and time.

## Scope

Initial tools: Age Calculator, Date Calculator, Days Between Dates, Add Days, Subtract Days, Working Days Calculator, Day of the Week Calculator, Week Number Calculator, Leap Year Calculator, Countdown Calculator, Time Difference Calculator, and Time Zone Converter.

Every tool must solve a recognizable task, explain its methodology, state assumptions, and link to related tools or guides. DatePilot prioritizes useful depth over hundreds of thin pages. Original content, accessibility, performance, and trust come before monetization.

## Non-goals

Do not create unrelated calculators, mass-produced keyword pages, copied content, or pages whose primary purpose is advertising. AdSense approval is never guaranteed.
