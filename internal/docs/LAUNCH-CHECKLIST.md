# DatePilot Launch Checklist

- [ ] Home and category pages clearly explain DatePilot.
- [ ] Core tools work on normal, boundary, invalid, leap-day, month-end, timezone, and DST cases.
- [ ] Every published page has original content, an example, methodology, and useful related links.
- [ ] About, Contact, Privacy, and Terms pages are complete.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard, focus, labels, errors, landmarks, contrast, and reduced motion are checked.
- [ ] Build, metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots, structured data, 404, and links are checked.
- [ ] Timezone data source and update process are documented.
- [ ] No prohibited content, artificial traffic, click encouragement, thin pages, or intrusive ads.
- [ ] Human reviewer records the final inspection before any AdSense application.
